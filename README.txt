HOUSING ASSISTANCE COUNCIL | TWO-PAGE RFP DESIGN PROTOTYPE

Extract the ZIP and open index.html in a current browser. Select Resources
in the header to open research-resources.html. The prototype requires no
build or server. External publications, tools, newsletter signup, and web
fonts require internet access.

The template's section order, hero carousel, card grids, tabs, accordion,
filter area, contact section, and footer layout are used throughout.
The resources page includes nine sample records from HAC's public
collection. Its search, filters, sorting, and previews work locally.
The research data tool links lead directly to the tools. The newsletter
opens HAC's hosted signup form.

The day/night and accessibility icons are decorative. The motion controls
for the homepage carousel and muted housing footage work separately.
The palette keeps the supplied logo and favicon intact and uses more
vibrant accents in the interface. The stock imagery is illustrative.
